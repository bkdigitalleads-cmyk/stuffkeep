Frequently asked questions

Why do I need a home inventory for insurance?
Because claims are paid on what you can document, not what you owned. A dated photo inventory with values and serial numbers turns "we lost everything" into a line-item claim.

What should a home inventory include?
A photo of each item worth claiming, its room, its value, and the serial or model number for electronics and appliances. StuffKeep captures all of it per item.

Where should I keep the inventory?
Keep a copy outside your home — email yourself the PDF or drop it in the cloud — so it survives whatever your stuff doesn't. The inventory itself stays on your phone.

Is it free?
Your first 30 items (with a photo each) are free. StuffKeep Pro unlocks unlimited items and photos and the PDF/CSV reports — one-time Lifetime purchase or Yearly.
