StuffKeep Privacy Policy

Effective August 20, 2026

StuffKeep is built so that your home inventory is nobody's business but yours.

What we collect: almost nothing

Your items, photos, values, serial numbers, rooms, and notes are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded, synced, or transmitted anywhere by StuffKeep. There are no accounts and no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Purchases

If you buy StuffKeep Pro, the purchase is processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat solely to validate purchases and restore them; RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier and purchase history, never your inventory data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for details.

Camera & photos

Camera and photo-library access are used only to attach photos of your belongings and to scan barcodes/serial numbers. Images are stored on-device only.

Data deletion

Settings → Delete all items & photos erases everything instantly. Deleting the app does the same.
